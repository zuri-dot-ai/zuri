About Zuri

Built by someone who watched good businesses stay invisible.

Zuri started with a simple observation: some of the best businesses we know — tailors, roasters, leather workers, boutique studios — had incredible products and no way to be found online, no plan for what to post, and no way to stay consistent once the initial excitement wore off. Zuri exists to close that gap.

Our Mission

Every African SME deserves a real, well-run digital presence.

Most small businesses across Nigeria run on WhatsApp status updates and Instagram DMs — not for lack of ambition, but because the tools available were never built for them: expensive, generic, and disconnected from how they actually operate. Zuri is different. Through a guided setup, it generates a premium website, hands you a 90-day action plan and content calendar tailored to your business, and keeps you accountable with a growth tracker — so showing up online stops being a full-time job you can't afford.

What Zuri Actually Does

Not just a website — an operating system for staying visible.

A premium website

Generated from guided onboarding, editable in Website Studio — no code, no designer, no waiting weeks. Preview free; publish from Pro.

A 90-day plan & calendar

AI-prepared captions, images, blogs, and newsletters in your content calendar, plus an action plan so you always know what to do next.

A growth tracker + agencies

Streaks and consistency tracking keep you accountable. On Growth and Premium, contact agencies in the marketplace when you need human help.

How We Think

Premium by default
