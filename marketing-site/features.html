Features

Everything to stay visible — without hiring a team.

Zuri is an operating system for your digital presence: a premium website, a content calendar, a 90-day action plan, and analytics that grow with your plan.

01 — Website

Website Studio

Generate a premium site from guided onboarding, then shape every section yourself — no code, no waiting on a designer.

  • AI-generated site matched to your business category and brand feel
  • Edit copy, images, links, embeds, and theme in Website Studio
  • Preview on Free; publish live to yourname.buildzuri.com from Pro
  • Custom domain and branding controls on Growth and above
02 — Content

Content calendar that knows what to post next

Stop staring at a blank caption box. Zuri prepares a monthly calendar of ready-to-use assets — so showing up online becomes a checklist, not a side hustle.

  • Captions and hashtags for your connected platforms
  • AI-generated images for posts, carousels, and stories
  • Blog posts and newsletters you can export and publish
  • Video scripts for reels and shorts (rendered video is not included yet)
  • Platform limits follow your plan — Pro and Growth support four platforms; Premium unlocks all
03 — Plan

90-day action plan & progress tracker

Visibility dies when momentum dies. Your Plan view breaks the next ninety days into concrete tasks — with streaks and badges that keep you honest.

  • Personalized 90-day action plan generated after onboarding
  • Weekly tasks you can complete and track
  • Streaks, badges, and consistency on your dashboard
  • Today’s next action surfaced on Home so you never wonder what to do
04 — Analytics

See what’s working — at the right plan level

Measure presence without duct-taping five dashboards together. Analytics unlock as you grow.

  • Pro: website analytics — pageviews, visitors, form submissions, top pages and sources
  • Growth: Meta (Facebook & Instagram) insights plus Google Search Console
  • Premium: longer retention and a monthly performance report
